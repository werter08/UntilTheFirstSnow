# Credits — Until the First Snow

Made for Mini Jam 221: Autumn (limitation: *Staying Warm*).

No generative AI was used for art or audio.

## Art

Hands (all poses, grey and beige sweaters), window frame, curtains, curtain rod, window sill and their shadows, counter (tabletop and drawers), wall with tiled backsplash, fridge with magnets and shopping list, electric kettle (parts, base, switch, hand-held pour poses), mugs (chipped, his, hers, two decoys), drink icons, V60 dripper, hand grinder, milk jug, frother and milk foam, jars (instant, cocoa, cinnamon), wall clock, calendar, ceiling lamp, window-sill plant (three stages), the courtyard (opposite building, Mira's window in five states, the chestnut tree through autumn), the front-door scene (door, Mira, her cup), coffee bag, spoon, weather particles (leaf, snowflake, raindrop) — drawn by the team. Shadows, mug outlines and the finger overlay at the door are cut mechanically from the team's own drawings; honey and salt jars are tinted copies of the drawn cocoa and instant-coffee jars until their own drawings exist. Some sweater variants are exact palette swaps of the originals.
